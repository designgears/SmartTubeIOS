# Profiles: one person, every service

Date: 2026-11-19. Branch: `tvos-overhaul`.

## Goal

"Who's watching?" picks a **profile**: a person with their own YouTube accounts, Twitch accounts
and Kick follows, and their own settings, history, watched marks and follows. A profile's pages
show all three services blended. Decisions (2026-11-19):

- A profile is a person; today's Google accounts become profiles.
- Home, Subscriptions, Channels and Live Now blend YouTube, Twitch and Kick (service badges),
  live first, then newest. The Twitch and Kick tabs stay for one service.
- Several accounts of one service in a profile are all active together: their subscriptions,
  follows and live streams are combined. One per service is the **main** account: playback,
  watch history, likes and Watch Later go to it.
- Kick: per-profile follows on the device now; real Kick sign-in later through a phone page.

## Constraints (account map, 2026-11-19)

- One live YouTube session per process (`AuthService`): one token, `ActiveChannel.shared`, the
  shared cookie jar, BotGuard/WebKit state. Keep it: it is the profile's **main** YouTube account.
- On-device data is swapped per **slot** by `AccountScope.activate` (settings, local history,
  watched, Watch Later membership, video states, search history, local subscriptions, queue,
  RSS, Continue Watching). The slot becomes the profile id.
- Only the live account's token is refreshed today.

## Design

- **`AppProfile`** (Core): `id`, `name`, YouTube account keys (`SavedAccount` id plus optional
  Brand `pageId`), main YouTube key, Twitch account ids, main Twitch id. **`ProfileStore`**
  (device-wide): the profiles, the active one, hidden ones.
- **Migration**: every current "Who's watching?" entry (account or Brand channel) becomes a
  profile whose **id is its current slot**, so its data stays where it is; Guest stays `guest`.
  New profiles get `profile.<uuid>`.
- **Switching** a profile activates its slot and makes its main YouTube account the live
  session (or none). Adding a YouTube account to a profile signs it in without switching the
  slot.
- **Other YouTube accounts** of the profile: their tokens refreshed on their own (a refresh
  helper over `SavedAccount.refreshToken`, written back to the vault), each read through its own
  `InnerTubeAPI` (own `ActiveChannel` for its Brand page, ephemeral URL session) — subscriptions
  feed, subscribed channels, live. Bearer-only TV requests, so no cookie or BotGuard sharing.
- **Twitch accounts**: device-code sign-in (`TwitchAuth`, scope `user:read:follows`, the app's
  public Client ID), tokens in the Keychain, refresh; Helix `channels/followed`,
  `streams/followed`. Several per profile.
- **Kick**: `ExternalFollowStore` joins the per-slot stores (follows per profile).
- **Blending**: one `ProfileFeed` merges the profile's sources per page (YouTube accounts,
  Twitch accounts and follows, Kick follows), de-duplicated, live first then newest.

## Phases (each: tests on Linux via x86sim, simulator check, commit)

1. `AppProfile`, `ProfileStore`, migration, slot = profile; switching drives `AuthService`.
2. "Who's watching?" lists profiles; a profile page: rename, add/remove accounts per service,
   pick the main one, delete.
3. Other YouTube accounts: token refresh, their own API, merged Subscriptions / Channels / Live.
4. Per-profile Twitch/Kick follows; Twitch sign-in (needs the Client ID), several accounts.
5. Blended Subscriptions, Channels and Home.
6. Kick sign-in through a phone page (needs a registered Kick app and a hosted page).
